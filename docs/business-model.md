# Business Model: Electrical Mechanics and Fitters Service Coordination Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-7412`
- ISCO-08: `7412`
- Occupation: Electrical Mechanics and Fitters
- Social impact: electrical-safety, equipment-reliability, service-continuity

## Customer

- electrical mechanical and fitting service contractors
- independent electrical mechanic/fitter crews / service cooperatives

## Offer

- technician/service-call shift/task scheduling coordination
- service-call/parts-usage/diagnostic-status record logging
- electrical-parts supply-order coordination
- safety-concern surfacing to certified electrical safety officers

## Revenue

- monthly retainer
- per-crew coordination fee

## Trust Controls

- no direct finalization of an electrical-repair-execution decision
  (e.g. deciding to proceed with a specific electrical repair), ever
- no authorization or finalization of a lockout/tagout-clearance
  decision, ever
- no override or bypass of an electrical safety officer's judgment,
  ever
- flagged safety concerns (arc-flash risk, lockout/tagout status,
  equipment hazard) always route to human sign-off, regardless of
  confidence
- no supply order above the registered cost threshold without
  governor-gated human sign-off
- operating and coordination records are auditable, not editable
